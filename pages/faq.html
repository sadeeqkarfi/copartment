About Copartment

Copartment was designed to help people in transition find safe, transparent and practical accommodation. We focus on the needs of students, interns, NYSC members and young professionals who often need flexible, trustworthy housing while building their careers and education.

The platform is designed to connect verified hosts with people who need reliable, short- to medium-term accommodation. Our vision is to create a trusted, practical, and accessible housing solution in Nigeria.
